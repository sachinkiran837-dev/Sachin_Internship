## What we decided

The published shape:

- **Three modes:** Strategic Advisory & Alignment, Build & Deploy, Managed Agentic Service.
- **The AI Transformation Playbook:** four phases, twenty components (Diagnostic & Strategic Alignment, The Quick-Win Engine, Scaling the Core, Enterprise-Wide Mobilisation).
- **The Rapid AI Diagnostic:** a 90-minute executive self-assessment across the twenty components.

How it connects to the system:

- The **flywheel (M3) is the engine inside Build & Deploy.**
- The **guardrails (M4) become AI Risk & Assurance (M9)** on the client side.
- The **Knowledge Layer POC Accelerator (M7)** is the productised Quick-Win Engine.
- The ownership split holds: the practice lead owns the P&L (selling, staffing, commercials); the AI Strategy Lead owns the method and the assets.
