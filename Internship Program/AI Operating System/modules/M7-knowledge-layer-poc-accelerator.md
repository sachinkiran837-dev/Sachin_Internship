**Scope (one line):** the productised six-week build that turns a client's data into a governed knowledge agent, the flagship expression of the flywheel and the context-layer method.

## What we decided

This is built and is the lead Surface 2 asset. The job is to confirm it as the productised form of the flywheel (M3) and to align its gates with the guardrails (M4).

What exists:

- **The playbook:** the six-week shape, the delivery team, the why-these-fail analysis, the reference architecture.
- **The toolkit:** Ontology Canvas, Golden-Question Eval Scorecard, Extraction Prompt Library, Permissions Map, Adoption Checklist, Demo and Handover Script.
- **Four gates**, including the permissions trust boundary that decides whether an enterprise rollout survives.

How it connects:

- It is the **Deliver stage (M3) made into a product.**
- Its permissions trust boundary is the **Gate 2 cyber discipline (M4)** applied to a real build.
- The context-layer it produces is the **leave-behind (M8).**
