The method, in plain terms

The machine does the mechanical work fast: it scaffolds a branded, running app from a single-file demo, wires the plumbing, and pins the numbers the client has already seen. That is the part a machine genuinely does better.

The judgement stays human. Whether to build at all, what the tool must think, whether the output can face a client, and what gets carried into the next build are set by people who have done this before. The engines in ink are where a Project Partner earns their keep.

The one thing to remember

The measure of the whole machine is the last engine: run N+1 is cheaper than run N. Every build routes its friction back into the starter, the skills and the library, and leaves exactly one reusable asset behind.

An idea that skips the front of the line, scoped as a demo by default and later wanted as more, forfeits a week of plumbing to recover. That is why the first engine is a person asking one question out loud.
